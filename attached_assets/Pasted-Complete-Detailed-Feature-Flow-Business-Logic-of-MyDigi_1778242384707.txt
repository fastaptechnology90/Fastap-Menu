Complete Detailed Feature Flow & Business Logic of MyDigiMenu
MyDigiMenu is a complete restaurant digital dining ecosystem designed for restaurants, hotels, cafés, bars, food courts, beach clubs, lounges, and room service operations. The platform combines QR menus, tablet menus, online ordering, CRM, loyalty systems, marketing automation, campaigns, reservations, guest engagement, and payment handling into one system.
